Good to know

Trustworthy by design. Every prediction shows its envelope status. Requests outside the trained pumping range are rejected, not extrapolated.
Speak instead of type. Use the microphone button to dictate a question. Review the text before sending.
Policy-aware. When you run a scenario, relevant Nebraska groundwater regulations are surfaced alongside the result, with sources to review — planning context, not a legal determination.
No setup to start. Most planning tools run without account setup. Regulations Q&A may depend on external service availability.
For planning, not advice. This is a planning and research demonstration, not legal or operational advice.
